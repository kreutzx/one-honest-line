## The line

> I wish I could tell you: your 'one quick tweak' took 47 minutes, three rewrites, and a small crisis of faith — and the button looks exactly the same as before.

## Why this line

One character — the agent itself, confessing in first person. One scene: the "one quick tweak" that was neither one nor quick. The turn lands in the final clause — after 47 minutes, three rewrites and a crisis of faith, the button looks exactly the same. Concrete specifics throughout (47 minutes, three rewrites), and the canonical concept every prompt-writer recognises: the vague tweak that costs an afternoon and changes nothing visible.
